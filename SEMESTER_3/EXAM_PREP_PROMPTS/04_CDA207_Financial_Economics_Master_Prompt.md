## 4. ERRATA / CURRENCY NOTES — show the deck value as the exam answer, plus a "📎 Current rule" footnote

Footnote: RBI's current Master Direction specifies a minimum of **₹5 lakh and multiples thereof** (verify). **CP issuer eligibility:** the deck quotes both a TNW of ₹4 cr (an older norm) and a ₹100 cr net-worth criterion; state the current rule in a footnote.

5. **Public company "needs certificate to commence"** (Forms deck): this is outdated. Since the 2015 amendment, every company with share capital files a **declaration of commencement (INC-20A, Sec 10A) within 180 days**, as the Formation deck states. Teach the current rule and explain the contradiction.
6. **Debentures** are described as "unsecured bonds" on the types slide, but the same slide lists secured debentures. Clarify: debentures may be secured or unsecured; in Indian usage the term is the umbrella for corporate debt.
